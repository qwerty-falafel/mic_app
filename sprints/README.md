## Foundation record

Sprints 01–07 remain the historical foundation. They established the database, outbox, worktrees, execution transport, resource checks and first browser checkpoint. Their fixed MIC lifecycle and one-shot planning/Build Auto mapping are superseded by the active epic; they are evidence of what was built, not the design target for future work.
